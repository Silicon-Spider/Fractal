## The question for ftxi's author, drafted

For Fergus to file as an issue on ftxi/5dchess_engine. When it is filed, its URL takes the place of `unreported: ...` in KD-0171 and KD-0172.

> **Title:** Standard - Half Reflected: which side's king and queen are exchanged?
>
> At v0.5.2 the variant table sets up `Standard - Half Reflected` with White's king on d1 and queen on e1 (`docs/branched-5dpgn.md`: "Standard board with White's king on d1 and queen on e1"). The sources I can find exchange Black's instead, leaving White as in Standard: `rnbkqbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR`.
>
> - The 5dpgn specification's own example game on this board (README, Shad Amethyst v PseudoAbstractMeta, 2021.01.22) plays `4... Qh5` after `1... e5 2... f6`, which is a queen from e8, and later `16. (0T11)Qd1>(2T11)f3`, which is White's queen from d1. Given that game's first four moves, v0.5.2 refuses `4... Qh5` ("Invalid move").
> - cwmtt's test record `tests/5dpgn/test1.5dpgn` (`[Board "Standard - Half Reflected"]`) plays `5. (0T5)Qd1>>(0T3)f3~`, and later `(1T6)Kd8>(0T6)c8` and `(0T7)Qe8>>(1T6)e7~`. v0.5.2 stops at move 5 for the same reason.
> - cwmtt, 5d-chess-js and the notation repository's parser all set the board up with Black's king on d8 and queen on e8.
> - The Steam guide "All Variants Explained (With Pictures)" (id 2223803379) shows the same board in its image, as I read it.
>
> Is the White-side exchange intended, or should it be Black's?
